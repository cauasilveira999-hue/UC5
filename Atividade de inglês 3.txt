1. Estudar
Afirmativa: I am studying for my exams.

Negativa: I am not studying for my exams.

Pergunta: Am I studying for my exams?






2. Trabalhar
Afirmativa: You are working hard today.

Negativa: You are not (aren't) working hard today.

Pergunta: Are you working hard today?
